# BCA Student Study Planner & Online Examination System

A comprehensive, responsive web application built with **React**, **TypeScript**, and **Tailwind CSS**, designed specifically for Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) students and academic administrators.

## Features & Modules

### 1. Student Academic Dashboard
- **Scheduled Exam Countdown**: Live dynamic countdown timer tracking the exact days, hours, minutes, and seconds until the next scheduled exam from the database.
- **Study Progress Analytics**: Interactive radar charts and concentric progress rings visualizing subject and topic mastery across BCA semesters.
- **Academic KPI Cards**: Total study tasks, pending assignments, completed exams, and overall assessment averages.
- **Today's Schedule**: Daily study timetable with priority tags, start/end slots, and instant task completion toggles.

### 2. Timetable & Study Planner
- Daily and 7-day weekly schedule manager.
- Full task CRUD with priority levels (High, Medium, Low) and topic tracking.
- Study reminders and deadlines.

### 3. Online Examination System
- Timed examination engine with active question palette (Green for answered, Yellow for review, Red for unanswered).
- Anti-cheat tab switch monitoring and alert warnings.
- Question-by-question review with score breakdown, percentage, pass/fail status, and confetti celebration on passing.

### 4. Study Notes & Academic Repository
- Syllabus, unit notes, previous question papers, and lab manuals organized by BCA Year and Semester.
- In-browser markdown preview and download capabilities.

### 5. Administrative Console
- Live student enrollment manager with account status toggles.
- Subject curriculum manager and examination creator.
- Question bank builder with marks, negative marking, and explanations.
- One-click CSV reports for student rosters and examination attempts.
